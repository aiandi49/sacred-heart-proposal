Dear Sisters of Sacred Heart,

We come to you with respect, humility, and a genuine desire to serve your community. Just Solutions is a local company that provides hands-on services — from masonry and concrete work to power washing, flooring, and grounds upkeep. We are your neighbors, and we care deeply about the people and places that hold this community together.

We recently learned that the Sisters are looking to sell the open area adjacent to the property. We would be honored to assist in marketing that land — creating visibility with local developers, investors, and community organizations — at no cost to Sacred Heart. We have already captured drone footage of the site and prepared this proposal to help share its potential.

This proposal is simple and transparent. We ask for your trust in the form of referrals, and in return we offer meaningful support: a complimentary pilot fundraiser in Sacred Heart's honor, establishment of a named community fund, and our active help in finding the right buyer or developer for the open area.

With sincerity and gratitude,
Just Solutions

THE THREE-PART PROPOSAL
Clear, transparent, and designed to honor Sacred Heart's mission
I
Referral Partnership
Just Solutions respectfully asks Sacred Heart to refer neighbors, families, parishioners, and local contacts who may need quality exterior or interior services. These referrals help Just Solutions build honest work relationships in the community — and they become the foundation for everything that follows. There is no obligation or pressure; we simply ask to be introduced to people who need good, trustworthy work done.
II
Complimentary Pilot Fundraiser
Once referrals lead to paid projects that sustain the work, Just Solutions will organize and run a complimentary pilot fundraiser in Sacred Heart's honor. We handle everything — promotion, registration, volunteer coordination, and donation tracking. The Sisters choose the format: a car wash, a bake or book sale, a donation drive, or a community gathering. All funds raised go to Sacred Heart's chosen causes.
III
The Sacred Heart Community Fund
We will establish a named community fund — the Sacred Heart Community Fund — supported by Just Solutions and our partners. Distributions will be directed entirely by Sacred Heart to shelters, orphanages, clinics, and services for women and children in need. Full transparency reports on funds raised and distributed will be provided after every event or campaign.
